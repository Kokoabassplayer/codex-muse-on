# Latch safety-critical failures

Ordinary Active-gate changes such as focus loss, controller disconnect, or permission loss may recover automatically after successful revalidation. A failed hold release, failed filter restoration, or uncertain device state instead enters a Safety latch: Control remains Inactive, sends nothing, and shows a specific reason until explicit Retry revalidates every prerequisite. If this failure occurs during Quit, the app refuses to exit until Retry verifies cleanup. After an unclean prior exit, the next launch stays Enabled but enters `Safety latch — Previous session ended unexpectedly`; controls must be released and Retry must verify cleanup before Active can resume. A Disable request is persisted immediately even when cleanup fails; after Retry verifies cleanup, Control finishes Disabled and requires a new Enable. This favors a visible human recovery step over silent retry or an apparently successful recovery when the controller's safety state is uncertain.
